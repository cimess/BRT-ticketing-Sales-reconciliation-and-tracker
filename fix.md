Reconciliation Variance Alerts (Automatic Flags):
If a POS session is closed with a deficit (actual cash remitted is less than expected float), the system flags it automatically and logs a Shortage Expectation in shortage_amount. We can display a list or alert card for "Outstanding Shortages" so supervisors can trace who owes what.
Deposit Receipt Uploads:
When a supervisor deposits their cash-in-hand to the bank, they can submit a remittance and upload a photo/PDF of the bank deposit slip. This makes auditing the bank statement against supervisor deposits 100% transparent.
Aging Expectations / Overdue Tracker:
A small widget showing "Overdue Remittances" categorized by time (e.g., Overdue 1 Day, Overdue 2+ Days). This highlights ticketers who are holding onto float past their assigned deadlines.
